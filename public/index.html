Playing in under a minute

  1. Step 1

    Open the TV screen

    Create a room and put it on the big screen. A room code and QR code show up straight away.

  2. Step 2

    Scan & pick an avatar

    Everyone scans with their own phone, types a name and picks an avatar. Their phone is now the controller.

  3. Step 3

    Vote & play

    The room votes on the next game. The most-picked one starts, and the points start counting.
